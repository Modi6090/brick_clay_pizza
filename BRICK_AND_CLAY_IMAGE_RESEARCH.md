## 2. Image Verification Principles & Integrity Standard

1. **Zero Hallucinated Food Photos**: We do **NOT** pull generic stock photos of arbitrary pizzas or pastas and label them as Brick & Clay dishes.
2. **Strict Verification Hierarchy**:
   - `REAL_OFFICIAL`: Directly hosted on Brick & Clay's official domain (`brickandclay.in`).
   - `REAL_RESTAURANT_PUBLISHED`: Uploaded directly by Brick & Clay on verified delivery partner platforms (Swiggy / Zomato).
   - `MISSING`: Dishes with verified recipes and prices but without verified high-resolution food photography. The UI gracefully displays a neutral, editorial craft card with verified ingredient tags and a *"Photo coming soon / Verified recipe"* badge.
3. **Copyright & Commercial Usage**: All non-official references are marked `REFERENCE_ONLY` for the redesign concept pitch. High-resolution original master files should be requested from Brick & Clay prior to commercial release.
